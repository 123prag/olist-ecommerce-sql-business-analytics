# Presentation Guide

## Recommended five-slide client presentation

### Slide 1 — Executive summary

Include:

- business question
- analytical scope
- three to five evidence-based observations
- one or two key limitations

### Slide 2 — Marketplace performance

Possible visuals:

- order trend
- delivered-order share
- merchandise value trend
- payment-value context
- order-status distribution

### Slide 3 — Customers and retention

Possible visuals:

- unique customers
- repeat-purchase behavior
- cohort retention
- RFM segment distribution

### Slide 4 — Products, sellers, delivery

Possible visuals:

- category performance
- top products within category
- seller performance
- late-delivery rate
- review-score distribution

### Slide 5 — Actions and monitoring

Structure recommendations as:

```text
Observation
    ↓
Business implication
    ↓
Potential action
    ↓
KPI to monitor
```

Keep recommendations separate from descriptive facts.

---

## Presentation principles

### One message per chart

Do not overload charts with multiple unrelated questions.

### Show definitions

A dashboard user should be able to understand what a KPI represents.

### Keep the population visible

State when a metric uses:

- all orders,
- delivered orders,
- item rows,
- unique customers,
- seller rows,
- or another population.

### Explain limitations

The Olist dataset is historical, anonymised, and not a complete picture of a live marketplace's financial or operational system.

### Avoid overclaiming

In particular:

- do not call a proxy "profit",
- do not claim current performance from historical data,
- do not infer causality from delivery/review association,
- do not treat RFM segments as official customer classifications.
